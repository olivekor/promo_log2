💡
How to read the GMV metrics
These metrics are GMV-weighted, not partner-count based.
% GMV → total GMV Weight represented by BTU partners in this city.
% GMV in Promo → share of this city's BTU GMV Weight currently covered by a promo.
% GMV ≥30% → share of this city's BTU GMV Weight covered by a ≥30% OFF or 2X1 promo.
% GMV Prime → share of this city's BTU GMV Weight covered by a ≥10% OFF Prime promo.
% GMV +5% Prime → share of this city's BTU GMV Weight covered by a ≥5% OFF Prime promo.
Important: % GMV is not the city's total Glovo GMV and does not represent a share of total Poland GMV.
